## 3. Biosecurity, Dual-Use & Export Control Compliance

### U.S. Export Administration Regulations (EAR)
* **Jurisdiction**: United States Department of Commerce, Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS).
* **Classification**: **EAR99** / **Not Subject to the EAR** under **15 C.F.R. § 734.7** (*Published Information*) and **15 C.F.R. § 734.8** (*Fundamental Research*).
* **Public Domain Exemption**: The mathematical equations, algorithms, physical constants, and biochemical formulations implemented in BioLabCalc (including the Beer-Lambert law, nearest-neighbor nucleic acid thermodynamics, restriction enzyme cut sequences, standard bacterial growth equations, and buffer equilibrium constants) are publicly available, established scientific knowledge published openly in peer-reviewed scientific journals for decades.

### Biosecurity & Dual-Use Verification
* **No Controlled Select Agents**: BioLabCalc contains no sequence design routines, virulence factor predictions, pathogen enhancement protocols, or engineering pipelines for Select Agents or Regulated Biological Toxins (as defined by 42 C.F.R. Part 73, 7 C.F.R. Part 331, 9 C.F.R. Part 121, or the Australia Group Common Control List).

## 7. Data Privacy, Telemetry & Offline Guarantee

* **100% Offline Execution**: BioLabCalc executes entirely within the local Python runtime environment on the user's host machine.
* **No Telemetry or Tracking**: The software contains **no tracking beacons, telemetry pings, crash analytics, or remote network calls**.
* **Data Sovereignty**: Sequence data, primer designs, experimental formulations, and laboratory notebooks created by users are saved solely to local file storage and are never transmitted to external servers.
